Master of Business Administration

MBA Programme

The College offers an MBA programme affiliated to the University Of Madras,Approved by AICTE New Delhi with 6 Specializations namely :


- Human Resources Management

- Finance Management

- Marketing Management

- Systems Management

- Logistics Management

- Hospital Management

Admission Procedure

Government allots candidates for 50% of the seats through TANCET Counselling. Our TANCET MBA Counselling Code : 365 . The remaining 50% are alloted by the management using the below mentioned procedure :


- Weightage for 10th standard marks - 10%
- Weightage for 12th standard marks - 15%
- Weightage for U.G marks - 25%
- Weightage for Group Discussion - 25%
- Weightage for Personal Interview - 25%

NOTE : Eligibility Certificate issued by the University of Madras has to be submitted by Candidates who have completed their U.G from a university other than the University of Madras.
