Best Business School in Chennai
Meenakshi Sundararajan School of Management (MSSM) stands out as one of the top-ranked MBA colleges affiliated to Madras University. Our students have consistently achieved outstanding academic results, securing top ranks in the university year after year: For the 2018–2020 MBA batch, MSSM students secured the 1st, 2nd, and 3rd ranks in Madras University. In the 2019–2021 batch, our students achieved the 1st, 3rd, 4th, and 7th ranks. The 2020–2022 batch saw MSSM students claim the 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, 5th, 6th, and 10th ranks. For the 2021–2023 batch, our students attained the 2nd, 3rd, 4th, and 7th ranks in the university. This consistent academic excellence reflects MSSM’s commitment to quality management education and rigorous academic standards. As an institution affiliated with Madras University and approved by AICTE, MSSM offers a two-year, full-time MBA program that blends academic rigor with practical industry exposure. With a limited intake of 40 seats, the program ensures personalized attention and a vibrant learning environment.
