🎯 Role of Counselling

The evolving role of counselling focuses on guiding students to successfully achieve their academic goals while reducing student attrition. Counselors act as mentors and facilitators of learning, encouraging skill development and academic growth.

They help students build confidence, resilience, and clarity in their educational journey through empathy, rapport, and professional commitment.

📝 Responsibilities

  • Providing counseling and mentorship support
  • Offering academic and career guidance
  • Helping students make informed decisions
  • Supporting social, intellectual, psychological, and ethical development

📌 Counselor Services

Assess student abilities through tests and inventories
Clarify educational and career goals
Guide students to utilize college resources effectively
